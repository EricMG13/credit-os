Brand application · what this mock-up takes from the brand guidelines
The issuer library and run start page on the primary trio, with the brand's chart colours and type.
Taken
Navy, Ultramarine and White as the whole interface. Inter for page titles, text and figures at 140% leading on a 4px grid. One chart colour order for every chart. State colours taken from the Green, Orange, Melon and Neutral ramps at steps that pass 4.5:1 as text.
Left out
Secondary colours as large fills, the secondary trios, the gold palette, ticker stickers and the tagline: the guidelines place those in campaigns and product marketing, not client reporting. The condensed display face for page titles (owner, 12 Nov 2026).
To confirm
The chart order is read from the Color order slide, not from listed values. The issuer and its figures are invented.
