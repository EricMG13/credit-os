Bottom line

A sole-source aftermarket franchise carries a thinner, more cyclical original-equipment business.

The aftermarket earns most of the profit and is hard to displace. The risk is concentration: one engine programme and three customers decide whether growth arrives on time.

Limitations 2 See this module’s gaps in Evidence
  • Customer names and contract terms come from management presentations, not from contracts. Where a presentation and the annual report disagree the module used the annual report.
  • The share of revenue under cost pass-through clauses is not established for this run. The annual report describes pass-through as applying to “most long-term agreements” without a figure, the lender presentation gives a figure for the aftermarket only, and the two segment definitions differ, so the module did not combine them.
