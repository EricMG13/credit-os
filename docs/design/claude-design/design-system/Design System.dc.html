Judgement first, on a calm page.

One accent, large type, rounded white surfaces on a faint violet canvas. Colour carries meaning only together with a shape and a word. Only a live run loops.

This system supplies, everywhere

Colour values, typefaces, status shapes, the gap, withheld and blocked states, motion and the focus colour.

Sizes, radius, borders and shadow on this page apply only to pieces written here (card, tile, gap panel, gap badge, withheld bar, status shapes, skeleton, trace control) and to page layout and text. A card is page layout: it may hold a group of source components or a chart.

A source supplies, for its own component

Layout, sizes, radius, border widths, shadow and density, as its live page shows them. A source component is copied, not restyled.

Confirmed at review 2, with three refinements: page titles are this system's 36px and judgement prose its 16px; charts follow the source, with faint horizontal gridlines and the source's bar corners; each metric is its own card.

Navy on the two lightest steps, white on the three darkest. An empty cell stays white.

A chart over periods puts the periods on the x-axis, oldest on the left. Marks follow the source chart: faint horizontal gridlines, no vertical ones, and the source's bar corners: 6px top corners on a bar up to 32px wide, 4px on the 8 to 12px bars of a line-and-bar chart, and in a stack only the top segment is rounded. This system adds one axis, a 1px baseline, and a dash and marker per line. A missing value is never drawn at zero: its slot stays empty and reads n.a., and a line breaks there. Labels and legends are always text colours, never the series colour. Status colours are never used as series.

10

Rules from the owner's prompts

Typed by the owner between 10 and 13 Nov 2026. The design system file in the repository lists each with the owner's words, its date and where it is held.
01Findings show outcomes and links. Backend and audit material is always findable, on Evidence and in the trace drawer, and never sits among the analysis.
02A handoff is a database: not everything in it is shown.
03One design is not repeated for a grouping of different information. No flat tables.
04No table identifiers, module codes or method step identifiers on screen. A module is called by its name.
05Nothing written for another module is displayed.
06Presentation is chosen for the reader: what they need, how they use it and why.
07A chart over periods has the periods on the x-axis. A chart is drawn as a chart: the statement table has no trend column.
08A sentence leads to its sources through the trace control, never through a citation in the prose.
09Nothing on screen that does not help the reader.
10Modern product design, not enterprise business software. Only colours and logo are fixed.
11A source component copies its source's design, and its live page is read before it is used.
