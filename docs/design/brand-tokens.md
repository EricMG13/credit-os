### Rules the guide states

- Primary is always the main scheme. Navy is the anchor for content-dense pages.
- Secondary colours pair with Navy: two secondary, or one secondary plus one tertiary.
- Tertiary is for data series, diagrams and interface states (error, warning, success, hover).
- Charts: flat fills, thin axes, no gridlines, no 3D. Content is the hero; no ornament.
- Target WCAG 2.1 AA; every chart must also read in grayscale and for the two common kinds
  of colour blindness.

### Left open by the guide (the design system decides)

- Status colour values, hover and focus colours, text and background contrast pairs.
- The chart series order. Seen in the guide's pictures, not printed: Ultramarine, Green,
  Yellow, Violet, a dark teal, Reef. The design system fixes an order and tests it.
- Neon secondaries (Green, Yellow, Cyan) fail AA as text on white; they are fills, not text.

## Logo

A symbol of three stacked swoosh strokes forming an S, with a stacked wordmark. Versions:
white on Ultramarine, white on Navy, Ultramarine on white, Navy on white or light grey. Navy
logo on inner pages, Ultramarine on covers. The guide set has no clear-space, minimum-size or
misuse page. The only vector file to hand is the predecessor's `brand/symbol.svg` (symbol
only); the wordmark carries the company name and is not committed.

## Type

- Headlines: Sharp Grotesk Bold 14, leading 95 percent, letterspacing -1.5 percent, sentence
  case with punctuation. It is a commercial font: its files are not committed here. Until the
  owner supplies a licence and files outside the repository, headlines use Inter.
- Subtitles, body, captions: Inter (Light, Regular, Medium), leading 140 percent.
- Fallback: Arial. Regional: Noto Sans.
- Sentence case everywhere, with the guide's exceptions (acronyms, names, navigation items).

## Space and surface

- 4 unit grid: every margin, padding and line step is a multiple of 4.
- Light grey panels (Neutral L9 `#F4F4F5`) on white, hairline rules, no shadows stated,
  rounded tiles. Radii, elevation, border widths, icons, buttons, forms and motion are not
  defined by the guide.
